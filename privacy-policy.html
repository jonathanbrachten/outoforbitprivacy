Out of OrbitPrivacy Policy

Effective date: September 24, 2026
Developer: Jonathan Brachten (“we,” “us”)

Out of Orbit doesn’t collect, sell, or share your personal information. There are no accounts, and your progress stays on your device.

Information we collect

None. We don’t collect your name, email, location, contacts, photos, device identifiers, or usage analytics. The game works entirely on your device and doesn’t need an internet connection.

Information stored on your device

The game saves your progress and settings on your device so you can pick up where you left off. This includes upgrades, resources, crystals, colour choices, music and sound settings, tutorial progress, and simple play statistics such as voyages played and distance travelled. This data stays on your device and we can’t see it. Deleting the app removes it.

Save backup

The Backup Save feature creates a code you can copy. We never receive this code. It goes only where you choose to paste it.

Contacting us and bug reports

If you email us or send a bug report, we receive what you send: your email address, your message, and the report text. The report text can include your game progress, device and software information, screen size, and recent error messages. We use this only to reply to you and fix problems, and we’ll delete it if you ask.

Ads and purchases

The alpha version has no real ads or purchases. If we add them later, purchases will be processed by Apple, and we won’t see your payment details. Any ads would come from a third-party ad network, which may collect device information under its own privacy policy. We’ll update this policy before that happens.

Third-party services

The game doesn’t use third-party analytics, tracking, or social media services.

Children’s privacy

Out of Orbit is a general-audience game rated 9+ on the App Store. It doesn’t collect personal information from anyone, including children under 13. If you believe a child has sent us personal information, for example in an email, contact us at the address below and we’ll delete it.

Your choices

You can delete the app at any time, or use Settings > Restart Progress to erase your saved progress. To have us delete any emails or bug reports you’ve sent us, contact us at the address below.

Changes to this policy

If we change this policy, we’ll post the new version on this page with an updated effective date.
